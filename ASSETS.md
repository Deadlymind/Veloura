# Veloura concept imagery

All generated assets use the built-in image generation tool. They are illustrative product/packaging concepts for the clearly labeled demo catalog, not photographs of purchasable stock.

## Hero

Saved at `frontend/public/images/veloura-hero.png`.

Prompt: Create a premium photorealistic editorial still life for Veloura beauty website. Landscape 3:2 composition. Warm blush pink and ivory background, soft natural morning sunlight from upper left, elegant shadow from delicate dried botanical branch. Arrange on sculptural warm cream stone plinths: one frosted peach glass serum bottle with ivory dropper cap, an ivory jar with copper lid, a rose nude lipstick in rose gold metal case, a small amber bottle. Fine minimal copper brand label VELOURA and tiny SKIN RITUAL lettering on bottles; no additional captions or graphic overlays. Fresh nude peach rose petals in foreground. Art directed luxe beauty campaign, muted terracotta and cream, tactile matte stone, exquisite product photography, realistic materials. Composition fills frame but airy. No people, no certifications, no claims.

## Product photography

Saved at `frontend/public/images/serum.png`, `cream.png`, `lipstick.png`, `body.png`, `hair.png`, and `makeup.png`.

Shared prompt: Premium product photography for Veloura cosmetics ecommerce. Portrait 4:5 format. Entire product perfectly centered, occupies 65% of height, with ample empty margins, complete uncropped product visible. Seamless warm ivory and pale peach studio background, warm beige matte stone tabletop, soft natural side lighting, gentle realistic shadow. Rich copper detailing, warm soft neutral palette, premium beauty editorial photo, exquisitely realistic materials, very minimal and serene. No extra items, no clinical claims, no captions, no other branding. Matches an elegant blush and rose gold cosmetics brand.

Subject variations:

- Serum: a single elegant frosted peach glass serum dropper bottle, tall ivory dropper cap, narrow rose-gold collar, label VELOURA and tiny DEW DROP SERUM. A little peach rose petal on the side.
- Cream: a single low wide ivory cosmetic cream jar, brushed polished copper lid, label VELOURA and tiny THE DAILY CREAM. Soft botanical shadow behind.
- Lipstick: a single elegant nude rose lipstick in a polished rose gold metal case, cap standing beside it, fine engraved label VELOURA. Soft nude rose lipstick bullet.
- Body oil: a single amber glass body oil bottle with a minimal copper pump, label VELOURA and tiny BOTANICAL BODY OIL. One delicate dried leaf on the side.

Hair and makeup prompt: Premium photorealistic product photography for Veloura cosmetics ecommerce. Portrait 4:5 format. Entire object perfectly centered and fully visible, generous empty margins. Pale peach seamless studio background, warm beige matte stone tabletop, subtle soft botanical shadow, warm natural side lighting. Premium editorial beauty still life, copper details and cream hues, matches blush and rose gold cosmetics brand. No other props or items, no clinical claims, no captions or other branding.

- Hair subject: a tall elegant matte ivory shampoo bottle with a simple rose gold pump, fine copper lettering VELOURA and tiny HAIR RITUAL.
- Makeup subject: an open circular rose gold blush compact containing muted dusty rose pressed powder, a clean mirror inside the lid showing a warm softly blurred reflection, fine engraved VELOURA lettering on the compact edge.

The six concept photos are reused across demo products; final product-specific photos can be uploaded in Django Admin.
